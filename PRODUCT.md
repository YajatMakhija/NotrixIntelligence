# Notrix — product truth

Product truth only. Visual decisions live in DESIGN.md; per-route strategy lives in the surface briefs.

## What this is

**Notrix** builds **Company Brain**, an intelligence layer for a company's own knowledge.
It connects the documents, systems and conversations a company already runs, indexes them
with the source systems' permissions preserved, and answers questions with citations back
to the original document, record or thread. The same governed index backs agents that can
carry out multi-step work with human approval checkpoints.

**Unique mechanism:** permissions are captured at ingestion and travel with the content,
so retrieval is scoped to the asking user's real access rather than filtered afterwards.
Every answer and every agent run is traceable to its sources.

## Audience

Enterprise buyers evaluating an internal AI layer:

- **Security, IT and compliance** — the people who can block the purchase. They ask about
  permission inheritance, audit trails, data residency, retention, model retention terms
  and deployment boundary.
- **Function leaders** (engineering, sales, support, operations, leadership) — the people
  who feel the problem: answers live in systems nobody can search together.
- **Executive sponsor** — wants one workflow proven before a company-wide rollout.

The buying scene is a laptop in an office, in daylight, alongside a browser full of vendor
tabs and a security questionnaire.

## What is true

- 14 first-party connectors: Slack, Notion, Google Drive, Google Docs, GitHub, Jira,
  Salesforce, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint, Zendesk, Snowflake, SAP, AWS, Google Cloud.
- Custom ingestion via REST API, webhooks, and MCP.
- Capability set: knowledge layer, permission-aware retrieval, cross-source reasoning,
  agent runtime with approval gates, workflows that write back to systems of record,
  exportable run history.
- Deployment options: managed cloud, private cloud, customer VPC, and enterprise
  arrangements covering residency and model allow-lists.
- Security posture: encryption in transit and at rest, SSO, role-based access, permission
  inheritance, audit logging of retrievals and agent runs, configurable retention and
  deletion, zero-retention terms required from model providers, customer inputs never used
  for training.
- Commercial shape: fixed-fee pilot (one workflow, one department, a success metric agreed
  up front), annual enterprise, custom for regulated deployments.

## Never fabricate

Hard constraint on every surface, including illustrative product mocks:

- Customers, logos, testimonials, case studies, named references.
- Revenue, headcount, funding, growth figures.
- Performance or accuracy numbers, benchmarks, latency figures, "x% faster" claims.
- Certifications, attestations, audit results, compliance status beyond the table above.
- Partnerships, resellers, or integrations that do not exist.

Illustrative product content (the questions, answers and source names in the hero
workspace) is authored demonstration material and must be labelled as illustrative
wherever a visitor could mistake it for a real customer environment.
